Postal use of the 15h

The 15 h covered common rates of 1919–1920 (e.g. a 4 × 15 h franking for a letter of the 4th tariff period). Recording cancellations of the scarcer perforations B, C and E maps where these stamps were actually used.

Note: All usage data below – railway post offices and post-office lists – relate exclusively to stamps from printing plates 1 and 2.

Post offices documented on perforations G (11½:13¾) and H (13¾:10¾), plates 1–2

Perforation G — 11½:13¾ (7 copies: fields 55, 53, 34, 17, 92 of plate I; 42, 75 of plate II): the Praha 1 cancel is documented on six stamps, with legible dates 18 Feb 1920, 25 Feb 1920, 21 Apr 1920 and Apr 1920 (month only).
Perforation H — 13¾:10¾ (5 copies: the 97+98/I pair, a newly found pair and field 84/I): two legible cancels — Turnov, 15 March 1920 (a horizontal pair, so far the earliest recorded use of perforation H) and Hradec Králové, 27 April 1921 (84/I); the 97+98/I pair bears machine wavy-line strikes without a legible office.
