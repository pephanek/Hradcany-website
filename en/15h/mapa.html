Cancellation map — plates 1–2 (perforations B, C, E, G, H), 3, 4, 7, perf A and imperforate 5–6

The map shows only the scarcer combinations of printing plate and perforation — common material (e.g. perforation A, or B and C on plates 5–6, thousands of copies) is deliberately not mapped; for the scarce groups, by contrast, every documented place of use has documentary value.

An interactive map of the postal-use locations of 15h stamps: post offices documented on the scarcer perforations of plates 1–2 (B 11¾ blue, C 13¾ orange, E 11½:10¾ purple; source: the records in the Hradcany_15h GENERAL workbook), the scarce perforations G 11½:13¾ (pink) — Praha 1 — and H 13¾:10¾ (brown) — Hradec Králové and Turnov, legible cancellations of plates 3 (red) and 4 (teal) from the used-stamp cancellation census, the usage locations of the rare plate plate 7 (green), and unique records of postally used imperforate plate 5 and 6 stamps (black) — Košice, Rimavská Sobota, Štitáre, Plešivec, Lučenec, Brezovka and Malý Lipník (13 copies). Newly also the usage locations of comb perforation A 13¾:13½ on plates 5–6 (olive). Multi-colour pie markers = several groups from the same place; the number gives the count of copies. Click a marker for details including cancellation dates; dashed lines mark travelling post offices. Layers can be toggled in the top-right corner. The only place that could not be located: Schenkenhahn.
