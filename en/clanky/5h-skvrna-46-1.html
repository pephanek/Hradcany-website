5h light green – spot at 46/1 (the “grape cluster”)

A well-known smear occurs on the 5h light green (POFIS no. 3), stamp field 46/1 – known among collectors as the “grape cluster” (hrozen).

This production flaw was caused by a foreign object sticking to the printing plate during printing. Here too you can see the initial state, when the object is on the plate and the ink adheres to it – the result is a coloured (green) spot with a white border. The next phase is a white spot only: the object came off the plate, but the spot probably remained greasy and would not take ink. As printing continued, the smear gradually shrank.
