News

What has been added to the site — new articles, additions to the records and updates to the statistics. The news log itself is kept in Czech.

  • 31. 8. 2026 15h — New section Thin print with ink spots in Production flaws: ink that is too thin and unevenly spread gives a weak, faded impression, yet in the very same places of the design it sometimes collects into small, solidly red spots — with seven recorded copies and an analysis of why it is neither a blank spot, a wandering ring, a smear, nor a světlina.
  • 30. 8. 2026 15h — The Production flaws page now offers a complete overview of printing and paper flaws: Partly doubled print (5 items), Wandering rings (14 items), Smears (19 items) and Blank spots (4 items) under Printing flaws; Creased paper (24 items) and Splinters in the paper (3 items) under Paper flaws. Under Damage to the printing plate, both articles – the cracked plate at 11/II and the damaged frame at 60/VI – are now shown directly on the page instead of just linked. The index at the top now links straight to each subcategory, and the fixed cancel map now correctly shows places under individual plate filters too, not only under “multiple groups”.
  • 30. 8. 2026 15h — New acquisition: a piece with partially doubled printing (perforation A, plate 1 or 2), with a dated cancellation of 1 June 1920; the post office is not legible — this is the first documented dated example of this perforation on plates 1–2. Now featured on the Production flaws page and in the statistics (6,477 dated cancellations).
  • 30. 8. 2026 15h — New acquisition: an imperforate vertical pair (15h vermilion) from plate V, fields 6+16, cancelled Košice 4, 1 September 1920 – postally used imperforate plate 5 is scarce (now 10 dated pieces).
  • 29. 8. 2026 15h — New addition to the records: a piece with perforation E (plate II) with a dated cancellation of 10 March 1920; the post office is not legible. The statistics have been updated (6,474 dated cancellations).
  • 26. 8. 2026 15h — New section Perforation shift – how the shift of the perforating grid against the design arises, what degrees it takes and why it is not a plate flaw; with a gallery of 28 items (29 stamps), from barely perceptibly unequal margins to pieces where the perforation runs through the design and a strip of the neighbouring stamp is left at the edge.
  • 24. 8. 2026 15h — New additions to the records: a horizontal pair with perforation H (Turnov, 15 March 1920) – the earliest recorded use of perforation H so far – two pieces with perforation E, Hoštka/Gastorf (31 December 1919, a post office new to the E records) and Česká Lípa/Böhm. Leipa (September 1919), and eight imperforate stamps from plates V and VI cancelled Košice, Plešivec, Lučenec, Brezovka and Malý Lipník. The statistics (6,416 dated cancels) and the cancel map have been updated.
  • 26. 7. 2026 15h — New article – a postally used imperforate 15h from the sixth plate (Košice 2, 13 July 1920, field 34/VI). The 15h cancel map now also covers legible cancels from plates 3 and 4.
